In short

I'm a designer and product manager with more than 15 years building web products that inspire, recently at Kaiser Permanente, where I led product areas for an employee portal serving 233,000 people. Product is where it all comes together for me, considering my years of experience in content, website development and operations, project management, user experience, and design. On the side I run Wildsite Co., my design studio, where I play with color, font, and animations.

Other sites I've worked on include Wells Fargo, Tufts Medical Center, University of California San Francisco, and Hatch My House, a startup I founded and grew to 3K+ users and $3.5M in gift transactions.
